ABOUT SKILLPATH AI

Make your next move with clarity.

A student-focused tool to understand current skills, identify development areas and explore career directions.

PROJECT OBJECTIVE

Turn self-assessment into a practical learning direction.

Help students assess self-reported skills, understand gaps, compare career profiles, and find learning resources.

⌘

Student accounts

Password-hashed student accounts using SQLite.

◈

Skill gap analysis

Weighted gap analysis and readiness calculation for the best-matching career.

✦

Career matching

A scikit-learn nearest-neighbor model compares assessment scores with reference profiles in data/career_skills.csv.

↗

Learning roadmap

Career matches are ranked by cosine similarity, then resources are ordered by weighted skill-gap priority.

Limitations

The career profiles are illustrative reference targets, not empirical hiring data. The model uses one profile per career and does not learn from student outcomes; its match score is not a prediction of employment success. Self-reported skill ratings are not a validated practical examination.
